# Touch Game Privacy Policy

Updated: 9 November 2026

Touch Game works offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Finger positions are used only in memory to run the current round. They are cleared when the round resets, you leave the game, or the app loses focus. They are never saved or sent to a server.

The app does not retain a gameplay history or personal data. No consent or deletion request is required to play. Removing the app removes its local installation data.

Your chosen background is saved only on your device so it is remembered the next time you open the app.

If you contact support by email, your email provider transmits your message and address. This information is used only to answer your request. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address.
